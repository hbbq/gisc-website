# GISC publishes first optical signalling protocol

**28 August 2026**

General Infrared Systems Corporation has published the first draft of the **GISC Optical Signature Protocol (GOSP-1)**, defining the company's **Optical Signature Identification (OSI)** technology.

The specification establishes a standardized method for persistent optical identification using modulated infrared signalling and forms the technical basis for the **OSB-1 Optical Signature Beacon**.

GOSP-1 defines an optical identity as a deliberately selected and repeatedly emitted temporal infrared pattern rather than as a conventional transmitted numerical address or message. Revision A specifies the initial physical signalling model, signature framing, code-space requirements and receiver behaviour.

GOSP-1 Revision A is currently published with **Draft** status through the **Research or Development Department**.

No GOSP-compatible products are currently in production.
